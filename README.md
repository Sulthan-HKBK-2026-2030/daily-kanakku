Daily Kanakku (கணக்கு)
A simple, phone-friendly app to track daily home shopping, Goli soda sales and family income and expenses. Works in English and Tamil, with big pictures so anyone can use it.
Features
Buy: Four sections with picture tiles: Vegetables, Fruits, Groceries and Others (dairy, cleaning, personal care).
Weights: Pick 100 g, 250 g, 500 g, 750 g, 1 kg or more, or type any amount. Price is calculated instantly.
Prices: Set or change the price of every item by hand, every day. Add your own items.
Goli soda: Count sodas sold (+ / −). Income is worked out at your soda price (default ₹7).
Today: Income, spending and balance, plus the last 7 days.
Finance: Add other income and expenses (wages, rent, electricity, transport, mobile, medical and more). Daily and monthly totals.
Calculator: Simple manual calculator.
Sign in: Create an account with a phone number or Google email and a password. Accounts and data stay on the phone.
PDF bill: Save the daily bill as a PDF.
How to use
Open the app link in Chrome on your phone.
Tap Add to Home screen to install it like an app.
Create an account and sign in.
Each morning, update prices in the Prices tab.
Add purchases in Buy and sodas in Soda.
Check Today and Finance for the balance.
After midnight, tap Save PDF on the yellow bar.

Publish and make an APK
Upload all files to a public GitHub repository.
Turn on Settings → Pages (main branch).
Paste the Pages link into pwabuilder.com and choose Package for Android.
Important notes
All data is saved only in the phone's browser. Do not clear site data, and save PDF bills regularly as a backup.
Sign-in is local to the device. It is not Google or SMS OTP login.
PDF bills are in English only.
Starting prices are examples. Set your real prices.
